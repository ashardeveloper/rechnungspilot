# RechnungsPilot DE — Desk Research Protocol

Status: Active, no direct user interaction  
Date: 2026-07-30

## Research objective

Build the most defensible product model possible without interviews, surveys,
outreach, paid research, or user interaction. The result supports a portfolio
prototype; it does not establish product-market fit.

## Permitted research inputs

1. German federal and EU regulatory documentation.
2. KoSIT/XRechnung specifications, validator artifacts, and fixtures.
3. Public competitor documentation, demos, pricing, and help articles.
4. Public reviews used cautiously as anecdotal evidence.
5. Open-source implementations and issue trackers.
6. Synthetic workflows and fictional test data.

## Evidence classification

- **Verified requirement:** Directly supported by authoritative material.
- **Observed public workflow:** Demonstrated in public product documentation.
- **Anecdotal signal:** Appears in reviews or issue discussions.
- **Reasoned assumption:** Plausible but not externally validated.
- **Synthetic scenario:** Created solely for design and testing.

## Research questions

1. Which structured E-invoice requirements are explicitly mandated?
2. Which XRechnung version and validation artifacts should the prototype support?
3. Which invoice fields are required in the selected synthetic scenarios?
4. How do public competitor workflows create, validate, render, and store
   E-invoices?
5. Which behaviors are regulatory requirements and which are product choices?
6. Which security threats apply to uploaded XML and stored financial documents?
7. Which features can be demonstrated fully with local open-source tooling?
8. Which claims must remain assumptions because users cannot be consulted?

## Research rules

- Prefer primary and authoritative sources.
- Competitor marketing proves capability, not customer pain.
- Reviews are anecdotal and never converted into market percentages.
- Do not invent users, quotes, revenue, retention, demand, or time-saved claims.
- Do not perform contact, outreach, surveys, interviews, or usability sessions.
- Do not use paid reports, databases, APIs, or research services.
- Synthetic invoices use fictional names, addresses, identifiers, and money.
- Record source dates because standards and product capabilities change.

## Desk-research outputs

- authoritative-source evidence register;
- public competitor capability matrix;
- domain glossary;
- versioned standards-support matrix;
- synthetic persona and workflow;
- fictional invoice fixture catalogue;
- verified-fact versus assumption register;
- portfolio claim boundaries.

## Decision boundary

Desk research can justify a technically relevant, standards-driven portfolio
prototype. It cannot establish:

- product-market fit;
- willingness to pay;
- customer satisfaction;
- adoption or retention;
- real-world time savings;
- preference for replacement versus add-on software.

Those limitations must remain visible in the case study.
